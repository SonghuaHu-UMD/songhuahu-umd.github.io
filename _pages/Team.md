<span class="lang-en">Team</span><span class="lang-zh">团队</span>
======

<span style="color: #34495e; font-size: 1.15em; font-weight: bold;"><span class="lang-en">Mobility Sensing and Computing Lab (MSCL)</span><span class="lang-zh">出行感知与计算实验室（MSCL）</span></span>

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">
We are a young and energetic group at the ACE department, CityUHK, brought together by a shared interest in urban transportation. We come from a highly diverse background such as CS, DS, GIS, EIE, transportation engineering, etc. Our goal is to use <span style="color: #2980b9;">**ubiquitous sensors**</span> to reconstruct <span style="color: #2980b9;">**high-resolution human mobility at scale**</span>, and to understand how human mobility interacts with the environment. Our work has been supported by *HK RGC* and *NSFC*, published in *Nature Sustainability* and *PNAS*, and featured on MIT News.

Come and build the lab together with us! See the current opening: <a href="/Opportunities/" style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">2 fully-funded Ph.D. positions for 2027</a>.
