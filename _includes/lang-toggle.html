{% comment %} EN / 中文 switch, rendered into the masthead so it is on every page. Two buttons rather than one that renames itself: "EN" stays readable to the reader who wants English and "中文" to the one who wants Chinese, neither has to recognise the other language to find the way back, and the control keeps a constant width so switching never shifts the bar under the pointer. aria-pressed rather than a radio group: these are not a form field, they act immediately, and pressed/unpressed is exactly what a toggle announces. The class hook is .no-collapse -- jquery.greedy-navigation.js keeps items marked with it out of the overflow dropdown, so on a phone the switch is still in the bar rather than behind the hamburger. {% endcomment %}
